The problem

Your answers are buried in documents nobody can find when they need them.

Same questions, every week

New hires and account managers ask the same questions in Slack, and a senior person answers them again and again.

Guides exist, but finding the right paragraph takes too long

The information is written down. It's just hard to search, especially under deadline pressure.

Guessing creates mistakes

When the right answer is slow to find, people guess. Wrong answers reach clients, and the cost is rework and lost trust.

Answers you can check

An assistant that only answers from approved documents, and shows where each answer came from, lets your team act quickly and verify before sending.

How it works

From your documents to a working assistant in four steps.

01

Share approved documents

You send the SOPs, playbooks, and guides the assistant should use. We agree the file formats and limits on a call.

02

We index them

The documents are split into passages and indexed for search by meaning, not just keywords.

03

Your team asks

Employees ask questions through a simple chat interface connected to the assistant.

04

Answers with sources

The assistant returns the answer with citations to the original document passages, so the team can verify it.
