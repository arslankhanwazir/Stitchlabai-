FAQ

Questions before a build.

What is a private AI knowledge assistant?

A chat assistant that answers your team's questions only from your own approved documents and shows the source passage for each answer.

What do I need to provide?

The approved documents (PDF, Word or text). We agree formats and limits on a call.

Can we test it before committing?

Yes. Send about 5 of your documents and I'll build a small working sample so you can ask it real questions before the full build.

How do I know the answers are right?

Every answer cites the passage it came from so your team can verify it. If nothing relevant is found, it says so.

Where do our documents go?

Documents are used only to answer your team's questions. I name the AI provider and the storage location in the written scope before any payment.

How long does a build take?

It depends on the number and type of documents. I give a fixed delivery date in the written proposal before payment.

What does the $1,500 build include?

The assistant on your agreed documents, cited answers, a chat interface, and handover documentation. Integrations, SSO, custom permissions and large document migrations are scoped separately.

Are hosting and API costs included?

Hosting and model/API costs for ongoing maintenance are quoted separately, and the deployment environment is agreed before the build.

Who owns the code?

You receive the code and a short runbook.
