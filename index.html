01 — About

Most automation demos are toys. Mine ship.

I build the unglamorous middle layer between the tools a business already has — the CRM, the inbox, the spreadsheet nobody trusts — and the outcome they actually want. That means n8n pipelines that survive real data, backend services in FastAPI, and AI agents that know when to stop guessing and ask.

Two of my own products are live right now, not portfolio pieces: a car-photo studio tool used by dealers, and an exam-prep app used daily by real students studying for Pakistan's civil service exams. Alongside those, I've built and shipped automation systems for healthcare booking, B2B outreach, CRM data capture, agency onboarding, and — most recently — the full GTM pipeline behind Nodespark.ai: lead scoring, qualification, and a live on-page demo that runs the product instead of describing it.
