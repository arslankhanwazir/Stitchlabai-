Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

StitchLab AI is operated by Arslan Khan, based in Peshawar, Pakistan. Contact: arslankhanwazir@gmail.com.

1. What we collect

  • Sample requests and enquiries: your name, work email address, company website, team size, a description of your documents, and any message you send.
  • Booking requests: the name, email, and time you choose for a call, and any notes you enter.
  • Website usage: pages visited, device and browser type, approximate location (country or city level), referring site, and anonymised clicks and scrolling.
  • Client documents: only if you choose to send them for a sample or project (see section 4).

2. Why we use it

  • To send the sample you asked for, arrange and confirm calls, and reply to your enquiries.
  • To send follow-up emails about our services, based on your consent. You can withdraw it at any time.
  • To measure and improve the website, based on your consent where the law requires it, otherwise our legitimate interest in running a useful site.
  • To deliver and support paid projects.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it to build advertising profiles.

3. Cookies and analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to count visits and Microsoft Clarity to record anonymised clicks and scrolling. Both may set cookies or similar identifiers, and the data they collect is processed by Google and Microsoft under their own privacy terms. Clarity masks sensitive content by default. Where the law requires consent (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), these tools load only after you accept them. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. We do not use advertising cookies.

4. Client documents and AI providers

Documents you give us for a sample or project are used only for that work and are not used by us to train AI models. The AI provider and the storage location for each project are named in the written scope before any payment, and only the passages needed to answer a question are sent to that provider. Please do not send confidential documents until secure sharing has been agreed with us.

The demonstration on this website uses fictional documents about an invented company, Brightpath Media. No real client document is shown on this website.

5. Who processes your information

We use trusted service providers to run the business: Cloudflare (website hosting and security), Google (Analytics and Gmail), Microsoft (Clarity), Brevo (email delivery), Cal.com (call booking) and HubSpot (contact management). They process information on our behalf under their own terms. Some are based outside Pakistan, so your information may be transferred to and stored in other countries, including the United States and EU countries.

6. How long we keep it

Enquiry, sample report and booking records: up to 24 months after our last contact, then deleted. Project documents: deleted within 30 days of the end of the project unless you ask us in writing to keep them. Analytics data is kept according to each provider's retention settings.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to see, correct, delete or export your information, object to or restrict how we use it, or withdraw consent, by writing to arslankhanwazir@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days. Each marketing email we send includes an unsubscribe link. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Security

We limit access to your information, use reputable providers with encryption in transit, and limit what is sent to any AI provider. No system is perfectly secure, and we will tell you promptly if a security problem affects your information.
